Hi, I am Ole.

I recently completed my master's degree in Environmental Modelling at the Carl von Ossietzky University Oldenburg. 
My bachelor's degree is in Mathematics from the university of Bonn. 

I love thinking about the great diversity of phenomena that are generated by natural and human systems and how mathematical models can capture them. 
My main strengths are the numerical implementation and simulation of models in programming languages such as Julia, Python or Matlab and my mathematical training, which is about equal parts abstract and applied mathematics.

Mathematical areas: nonlinear dynamics, especially rate-induced tipping and chaos theory, network theory, stochastic processes and statistics.


A small selection of reports from my masters degree can be found in the Portfolio section.

### Master's Thesis
In my master thesis, I studied rate-induced tipping from a chaotic attractor in a three-species food chain model. 
 
While working on my thesis, I created some beautiful images which show the Lyapunov exponents of the food chain model. More details and images can be found [here](https://ole-sc.github.io/posts/2026/04/lyapunov-exponents).
